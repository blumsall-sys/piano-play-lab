HOW TO PLAY

Start with notes. Grow into chords.

1
Find the golden keys

One golden key is a note; several golden keys make a chord.

2
Play it on your piano

Use the microphone, tap the piano keys, or connect USB MIDI to recognize chord combinations.

3
Listen for the chime

Each correct note plays a success chime and moves you closer to the end.

This is a pitch-recognition experiment, not a grading system. Background noise or overlapping notes can confuse the microphone.
