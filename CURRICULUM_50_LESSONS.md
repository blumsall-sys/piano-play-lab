## Instructional progression

- **21–24:** Right- and left-hand five-finger positions, thumb/finger numbers, short patterns and natural hand movement.
- **25–28:** Quarter/eighth/half values, steady beat, steps/skips and finding B-flat.
- **29–30:** Alternating hands and two-hand octave pairs.
- **31–40:** Recognizable traditional/public-domain melodies and an original playful piece, all simplified within the toy piano range.
- **41–47:** Two-hand coordination with bass + melody pairs, hand changes and a chord under a right-hand pattern.
- **48–50:** Chord storytelling, a simple two-hand Twinkle arrangement and a final original beginner concert.

A song lesson is a **simplified teaching arrangement**, not a complete score or a precise transcription. The current Rhythm Highway shows note names and beats, not formally engraved sheet music.

## Recognition and fingering

Mic input estimates sounded pitches; it cannot validate which finger or hand played them. MIDI provides precise pitch-on/pitch-off and simultaneous note information, but not finger identity. The finger assignments are visual teaching references only.
